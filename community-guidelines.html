Community Guidelines

> Draft Notice: This document is a business draft for HandymanNow > and should be reviewed by legal counsel before production use.

Last Updated: 2026-08-05

These Community Guidelines are part of the Terms of Service and apply to your use of the HandymanNow app (the "App"), operated by HandymanNow.

1. Purpose

These Community Guidelines explain the standards of conduct expected from everyone using HandymanNow.

2. Treat Others with Respect

All users must communicate respectfully. Harassment, bullying, threats, intimidation, hate speech, discrimination, or abusive language are prohibited.

3. Honest Information

Only provide truthful information about yourself, your services, your property, and your job requests. Do not impersonate another person or business.

4. Lawful Use

Do not use the App to arrange illegal activities, request unlawful services, or violate applicable laws or regulations.

5. Professional Conduct

Handymen should arrive on time when reasonably possible, communicate professionally, perform only work they are qualified to perform, and accurately represent their experience.

Customers should provide accurate job descriptions, maintain a safe environment, and treat service providers respectfully.

6. Fraud and Abuse

The following are prohibited: - Fake accounts - Fake job requests - Review manipulation - Identity fraud - Payment fraud - Spam - Attempts to exploit App systems

7. Photos and Content

Only upload content you own or have permission to use. Do not upload illegal, offensive, violent, sexually explicit, or infringing material.

8. Messaging

Use in-app messaging only for legitimate communication related to services. Do not send spam, scams, or repeated unwanted messages.

9. Safety

If you believe a situation presents an immediate danger, leave the area if it is safe to do so and contact local emergency services.

10. Reporting

Users are encouraged to report suspected fraud, abusive conduct, unsafe behavior, or policy violations through the App.

11. Enforcement

HandymanNow may investigate reported violations and may: - Remove content - Issue warnings - Restrict App features - Suspend accounts - Permanently terminate accounts where permitted by applicable law.

12. Appeals

Where available, users may contact support to request review of an enforcement action. Submission of an appeal does not guarantee reversal.

13. Updates

These Guidelines may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Guidelines.
