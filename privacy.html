This Privacy Policy explains how HandymanNow ("HandymanNow," "we," "us," or "our"), located at an address available upon request via Contact Support, collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with the HandymanNow mobile application and related services (together, the "App"). It applies to Customers, Handymen, and anyone else who creates an account or otherwise uses the App.

By creating an account or otherwise using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. This Policy is incorporated into, and should be read together with, our Terms of Service and, where applicable, our Customer Terms, Independent Handyman Agreement, Cancellation Policy, Safety Policy, Community Guidelines, and Cookie Policy.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Policy covers information we collect through the App itself (the Customer and Handyman mobile experience, and our administrative web panel used by our team). It does not cover the practices of third parties we don't control, including a handyman's or customer's own conduct outside the App, or websites/services we merely link to.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number, password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider, never in plain text by us), and role selection (Customer and/or Handyman).
  • Profile information: a profile photo you choose to upload, and — for handymen — biography, years of experience, services offered, skills, service radius/area, availability, portfolio/work photos, and any licensing/insurance documents submitted for approval.
  • Job information: service category, job description, urgency, requested/preferred timing, photos attached to a request or a job (including before/after work photos), and the address associated with a job.
  • Saved addresses: any address you save to your account for faster future requests.
  • Communications: the content of messages you send through in-app chat related to a job or to Support, and any reports, disputes, or reviews you submit.
  • Support and feedback: anything you submit through Contact Support, Report a Problem, Report an Issue, or an in-app satisfaction survey, including any photos attached to a report.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location information: your device's precise or approximate location while you use location-dependent features of the App (see Section 4, "Location Information," for the specific rules that apply before and after a job relationship exists).
  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, general usage patterns within the App (such as which screens are used), and diagnostic/crash information, collected to keep the App reliable and secure.
  • Push notification tokens: a device-specific identifier used solely to deliver notifications to your device.

2.3 Information From Third Parties

  • If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive the basic profile information (such as name and email) that Google shares with us based on your Google account settings and your consent at the time of sign-in.
  • We do not currently purchase or receive information about you from data brokers or advertising networks.

2.4 Payment Information (Handymen)

HandymanNow uses Stripe (Stripe, Inc.) to provide payment-method setup and payment processing functionality in the App. This applies to handymen only — customers are not asked for payment information anywhere in the App.

What Stripe handles, not us. When a handyman adds a payment method, the card or wallet details are entered into Stripe's own payment sheet and are submitted directly to Stripe. HandymanNow never receives or stores full card numbers, expiry dates, or CVC/security codes. Google Pay may be offered as a payment method inside that Stripe sheet, depending on your device and Google account.

What we send to Stripe. The handyman's name and email address, and an internal account identifier, so that Stripe can create and identify the corresponding customer record.

What we store. Only the identifiers and status information needed to operate the account and the lead features:

  • the Stripe customer identifier associated with the account;
  • the payment-method status (not added, pending, active, or failed);
  • a Stripe-provided card fingerprint — a stable identifier that Stripe derives per physical card. It is not the card number, cannot be reversed into one, and cannot be used to charge a card. We use it for a single purpose: detecting the same card being used to claim the free-lead allowance on more than one handyman account;
  • counts relating to a handyman's free-lead allowance and leads received.

No amount is charged. Adding a payment method uses a Stripe SetupIntent, which stores the method for future use and authorises $0. Consistent with Section 3 of our Terms of Service, HandymanNow does not currently charge handymen or customers, collect commissions, or process payment for the work performed between a customer and a handyman.

Stripe processes the personal and payment-related information it receives under its own privacy practices. See Stripe's privacy policy at https://stripe.com/privacy.

We do not use your information to serve third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Location Information

Location is central to how the App works, so we want to be specific about exactly how it's used at each stage:

  • Before any job relationship exists: when a customer browses the map, a nearby handyman's position shown to that customer is intentionally approximate, not their exact live GPS position or exact registered base address — a deliberate, permanent privacy protection (not a bug or a temporary limitation) adopted specifically to prevent a customer from being able to track a specific handyman's exact real-time location before any job connects the two of you. The real distance and any ETA shown to the customer remain accurate; only the drawn map pin position is adjusted.
  • Once a job is accepted: the assigned handyman's exact live location becomes visible to the assigned customer (and to authorized administrative staff for support/safety purposes) for the duration of that specific job, most intensively while the handyman's status is "on the way," to support live tracking and arrival estimation.
  • While a handyman is travelling to a job: with the handyman's explicit permission, HandymanNow collects the handyman's location continuously while they are on the way, including when the app is in the background or the device screen is off. This is what allows the assigned customer to see live location and arrival progress. Collection in the background stops as soon as the handyman arrives, the job leaves the travelling state or is cancelled, or the handyman goes offline. Customers' locations are never collected in the background.
  • After a job is completed: live location sharing for that job ends. We do not continue to share either party's real-time location with the other after the job relationship ends.
  • Location is never visible to any customer or handyman other than the specific counterparty on a specific job, and is never used for advertising or sold to third parties.

5. How We Disclose Information

We disclose information only as described below. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

  • To the other party on a job: a handyman assigned to your job can see the job address, job details, and (per Section 4) your live location while the job is active. A customer can see their assigned handyman's profile information, approval/rating status, and live location while the job is active.
  • To our service providers: we use third-party vendors who process information on our behalf and under our instructions, strictly to help us provide the App, including:
  • our backend database, authentication, and file storage provider;
  • mapping, geocoding, and directions providers, to show maps and estimate routes/ETAs;
  • push notification delivery services;
  • Stripe, for payment-method setup and payment processing functionality (handymen only) — see Section 2.4;
  • Google, if you use Google Sign-In.

These providers are authorized to use your information only as necessary to provide their service to us, not for their own independent purposes.

  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.
  • In a business transfer: if HandymanNow is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection, with your consent.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most of your profile information directly within the App at any time.
  • Notification preferences: you can control which optional notification categories you receive from Profile → Notification Preferences.
  • Location permissions: you can control location access for the App at the operating-system level at any time; note that core features (finding nearby handymen, live tracking) will not function correctly without it.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account, provided you have no unresolved active job. Deleting your account deactivates it and stops further processing of your information for active-marketplace purposes; certain records (such as job history relevant to a past dispute, or records required for legal/tax compliance) may be retained as described in Section 7.
  • Depending on your location, applicable law may give you additional rights, such as the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, the right to request correction or deletion, the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You can exercise these rights by contacting us through Contact Support; we will respond within the time required by applicable law.
