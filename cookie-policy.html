Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

This Cookie Policy is part of the Terms of Service and applies to your use of the HandymanNow app, operated by HandymanNow.

Mobile App

The HandymanNow mobile app (iOS/Android) does not use browser cookies — cookies are a web-browser technology and the mobile app is a native application. The mobile app does store some information locally on your device (such as your session so you don't have to log in every time, and your language preference) using standard mobile app storage, not cookies.

Admin Web Panel

HandymanNow's administrative web panel uses only strictly-necessary session storage (an authentication token) to keep an admin signed in. It does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies.

No Third-Party Advertising Cookies

HandymanNow does not currently use any third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, on the mobile app or the admin web panel.

Changes

If this changes — for example, if a future web-based customer or handyman experience is introduced — this policy will be updated to accurately describe it before that happens.
